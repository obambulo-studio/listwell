# ≥ 20 Google reviews

Review volume shows you are established and gives Google more text to rank you on. We check for at least 20 Google reviews.

::tech-detail{summary="How we measure review count"} When `GOOGLE_API_KEY` and a Google Place ID are present, we read the official Google Places review count. If those are missing, we only use counts published on a pasted listing page or website schema. We do not invent review counts. ::

## What we're checking

We verify that your Google Business Profile has at least 20 reviews.

::impact{type="customers" severity="high"} **93% of consumers** read online reviews before visiting a business ::

## How can I fix it?

Use email or SMS follow-ups, QR codes at checkout, and staff prompts to ask happy customers for reviews. See Google's review policies before offering incentives.

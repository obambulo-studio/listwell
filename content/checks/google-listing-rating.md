# Rating ≥ 4.0

Your average Google rating is a quick signal of trust. We check whether your profile meets a 4.0 star target.

::tech-detail{summary="How we measure rating"} When `GOOGLE_API_KEY` and a Google Place ID are present, we read the official Google Places rating. If those are missing, we only use aggregate rating published on a pasted listing page or website schema. We do not invent ratings. ::

## What we're checking

We verify that your Google Business Profile shows an average rating of at least 4.0 stars.

::impact{type="customers" severity="high"} **89% of consumers** won't consider a business rated below 4 stars ::

## How can I fix it?

Respond to reviews, resolve recurring complaints, and ask satisfied customers to share feedback. See our full review guide in the other Google review checks in Listwell.

::time-estimate{minutes="480" difficulty="medium"}::
